Our Story

The Heart Behind the Technology

Orange Coeur is an independent web design and development business focused on creating modern, functional and responsive websites for businesses and organizations.

Founded on the belief that good digital experiences should be both visually engaging and practical, Orange Coeur combines frontend development, project coordination and an understanding of business operations.

We named ourselves "Coeur" (French for "heart") because we put the heart of your business at the center of every technological decision we make.

We are not just building a website or a technology company. We are building a business that helps other businesses turn ideas into working digital solutions.
